# Atlassian

Find and work with Jira issues and Confluence content through Atlassian Rovo.

## Access requirements

Sign in to Atlassian in your browser and approve the access you want to grant. Your account permissions and plan determine which resources and tools you can use.

Your Atlassian organization can restrict OAuth clients or loopback callbacks.

[Provider documentation](https://developer.atlassian.com/cloud/rovo-mcp/guides/authentication-and-authorization/)
